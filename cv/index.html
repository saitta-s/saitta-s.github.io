Profile

  • I develop data-driven methods to make cardiovascular imaging more quantitative and objective. My work brings together deep learning, computational modeling, physics, and image analysis to support diagnosis, treatment planning, and image-guided cardiovascular procedures.

Experience

  • 2024 - now
    Assistant Professor in Cardiovascular Image Analysis
    Amsterdam UMC — Dept. of Biomedical Engineering & Physics, The Netherlands
    • Manager of the CARA Lab (a joint initiative of Amsterdam UMC, Radboudumc and Abbott Vascular) — development and clinical validation of AI systems that analyse intracoronary optical coherence tomography (OCT) in real time during procedures in the catheterization laboratory.
    • Lead an independent research line on Marfan syndrome, combining medical imaging, biomechanics and machine learning to quantify a patient's risk of aortic-disease progression.
    • Deep learning for coronary plaque characterisation and detection of thin-cap fibroatheromas, and geometric deep learning as a fast surrogate for blood-flow simulation.
    • Visiting Researcher, Diagnostic Image Analysis Group (DIAG), Radboudumc.
